Output Names

HISTI replaces the 3840x2160 token with 1920x1080 and leaves the rest of the file name alone.

jep_and_jess_beyond_the_bayou_s01_e01_eng_bg_16x9_3840x2160.jpg becomes jep_and_jess_beyond_the_bayou_s01_e01_eng_bg_16x9_1920x1080.jpg.

Bulk Downloads

  • One ready image downloads as one JPG.
  • Multiple ready images download together as a ZIP.
  • Skipped files stay listed with the reason they were skipped.

Privacy

The browser version runs locally in the page. The images do not leave your computer.
